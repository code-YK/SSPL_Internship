## Preprocessing Plan for Automotive Price Prediction

### 1. Feature Cleaning & Dropping
- **Drop redundant columns:**  
  - `year` → Already represented by `vehicle_age`.  
  - `mileage_per_year` → Can be derived from `mileage` & `vehicle_age` (avoid leakage).  

- **Handle missing values:**  
  - `accident_history` → Fill missing values with `"No Accident"`.  
  - Other categorical columns → Fill with mode.  
  - Numeric columns (if missing) → Fill with median.  

---

### 2. Feature Engineering
- **Log Transform Target:**  
  - Create `price_log = np.log1p(price)` to normalize skewed target distribution.  

- **New Features:**  
  - `price_per_hp = price / engine_hp` → Price-to-performance metric.  
  - `is_luxury_brand = 1 if make ∈ [BMW, Mercedes, Audi, Lexus, ...] else 0` → Captures luxury premium.  

- **Condition Encoding:**  
  - Map `condition` to numeric: `Excellent=3, Good=2, Fair=1`.  

---

### 3. Categorical Encoding
- **One-Hot Encode (Low Cardinality):**  
  - `transmission`, `fuel_type`, `drivetrain`, `body_type`, `seller_type`  

- **Target/Frequency Encode (High Cardinality):**  
  - `make`, `model`, `trim`, `exterior_color`, `interior_color`  

---

### 4. Scaling
- Apply **RobustScaler** to numerical features to minimize outlier impact:  
  - `mileage`, `engine_hp`, `vehicle_age`, `brand_popularity`, `price_per_hp`

---

### 5. Outlier Handling
- Keep outliers, as they represent genuine expensive vehicles.  
- Scaling with `RobustScaler` will reduce their influence on model training.  

---

### 6. Train-Test Split
- Perform **Stratified Train-Test Split** (e.g., using `vehicle_age` bins or `make`)  
  - Ensures similar distribution of key features across train/test sets.  

---

### 7. Pipeline & Modeling
- Wrap preprocessing in a single **scikit-learn Pipeline**:
  - Imputation → Feature Engineering → Encoding → Scaling  
- Keep model step flexible to swap between:
  - Linear Regression, Decision Tree, RandomForest, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost  
- Evaluate on `price_log` target → Inverse transform predictions for final metrics.
